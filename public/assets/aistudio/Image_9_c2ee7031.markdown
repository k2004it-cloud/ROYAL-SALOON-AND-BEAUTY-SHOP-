## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end boutique salon atmosphere for commercial salon business operations. It combines the poise and prestige of editorial beauty publishing with the ergonomic rigor of modern enterprise point-of-sale software. The visual language blends high-contrast, graceful serif headers with crisp, functional geometric typography and deep, dramatic chrome surfaces.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Poised & Luxurious:** Clients and salon owners encounter an editorial, premium ambiance rather than sterile operational software.
- **Masterful & Decisive:** Touch-first POS interactions, scheduling matrices, and financial flows offer immediate visual clarity, speed, and confidence.
- **Curated Warmth:** Deep plum tones anchor high-frequency workflows, balancing luminous, spotless canvas areas that mirror a spotless studio environment.

### Design Aesthetic
The system unites modern editorial minimalism with subtle tonal layering. Key hallmarks include high-contrast typography, restrained hairline borders, ambient warm-tinted depth, and rich deep-purple chrome for primary navigation contexts.

## Colors

The palette balances clean, ultra-light canvas planes with rich, anchoring chrome tones and vivid royal violet accents.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#6D28D9` / `#7C3AED`):** Royal violet reserved for high-intent actions, active appointment selection states, touch-first POS triggers, and primary progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#DDD6FE` / `#EDE9FE`):** Soft lavender wash applied to hover highlights, calendar time-block backdrops, and active tab containers.
- **Tertiary (`#1E152A` / `#120D1A`):** Deep charcoal violet reserved for top bars, sidebar navigation chrome, POS drawer framing, and high-emphasis display badges.
- **Neutral (`#6B7280`):** Balanced cool-slate family bridging stark content separation. Canvas surfaces utilize pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and crisp studio grey (`#F9FAFB`), framed with gentle structural hairlines (`#E5E7EB`).

### Functional & Semantic States
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Emerald green for confirmed bookings, settled invoices, and cash drawer reconciliations.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber for stylist scheduling conflicts, low product inventory alerts, and pending deposits.
- **Error (`#EF4444`):** Crisp crimson for declined transactions, double bookings, and voided tickets.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between the boutique editorial front-of-house identity and fast-paced operational rigor.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Accents):** Bestows classic elegance on appointment totals, suite titles, client review quotes, and screen-level section titles. Never use below `1.125rem` to prevent illegibility in fine serifs.
- **Manrope (Interface, Data, Financials, and Labels):** High-clarity geometric sans-serif tuned for screen legibility. Used across grid schedules, tabular ledger rows, touch POS keys, and form elements.
- **Financial Formatting:** Currency figures (e.g., `KSh 14,500`) must be styled in Manrope with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align digits perfectly across ledgers and daily sales totals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances information density with generous whitespace to prevent cognitive fatigue during peak service hours.

### Layout Model
- **Primary Grid:** 12-column responsive fluid grid across desktop and tablet viewports, collapsing to a single-column or dual-pane configuration on mobile terminals.
- **Desktop POS & Master Schedule:** Dual-rail layout with a fixed, collapsed or expanded deep-purple navigation rail (80px to 260px), a flexible central viewport for scheduling matrices, and an optional 380px fixed-width contextual cart/ticket drawer.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Interactive targets within POS touch surfaces must maintain a minimum touch bounding box of 48px by 48px, with `0.5rem` minimum internal padding.

### Breakpoint Strategy
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Navigation transitions to a bottom bar or slide-over drawer; outer margin contracts to `1rem`; tables convert into card-based order rows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Split-view layouts active (e.g., service list on the left, running ticket on the right); grid uses `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full appointment diary view with multi-column stylist lanes and persistent financial telemetry.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on clean surface layering, crisp micro-borders, and low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with deep plum undertones.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#F9FAFB` provides a non-glare, warm-grey background for daily screen use.
- **Raised Surfaces (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` cards, calendar cells, and data tables separated from the canvas via a hairline border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 21, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 21, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Modals & POS Sheets (Level 2):** Booking dialogs, service pickers, and checkout summaries utilize an elevated shadow tinted with tertiary dark violet: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(30, 21, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 21, 42, 0.04)`, framed with `1px solid #DDD6FE`.
- **Navigation Chrome (Deep Layer):** Primary global navigation uses solid `#1E152A` or `#120D1A`, projecting visual weight through color contrast rather than drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined rounded shape language (`roundedness: 2`). This corresponds to a standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px), scaling gracefully to `1rem` (16px) for larger service panels and card groups, and `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent display tiles and checkout drawer headers.

### Shape Guidelines
- **Buttons, Inputs, and Badges:** `0.5rem` (8px) provides a structured, modern silhouette that pairs naturally with both Playfair Display headlines and Manrope UI text.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `1rem` (16px) for client records, product tiles, and analytics cards.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Fully rounded capsule styling (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for appointment status badges, chip filters, and numeric counter bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#6D28D9` background, white label, `0.5rem` border-radius, minimum height 44px (48px for POS quick-pay keys). Hover state deepens to `#5B21B6`. Active state uses `scale(0.98)` for tactile confirmation.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** `#EDE9FE` background, `#6D28D9` text label. Zero border. Ideal for secondary checkout choices or quick service add-ons.
- **Tertiary / Outlined Button:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, `#1E152A` label. Hover brings a subtle `#F9FAFB` tint.
- **Danger Button:** Pure white background with `#EF4444` outline and text, or solid `#EF4444` for irreversible actions (e.g., Void Ticket).

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small capsule indicators (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-sm` typography).
  - *Confirmed / Paid:* `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text.
  - *In-Service / Ongoing:* `#EDE9FE` background with `#6D28D9` text.
  - *Pending / Waitlist:* `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text.
  - *Cancelled / No-Show:* `#FEF2F2` background with `#B91C1C` text.
- **Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5E7EB`, transitioning to `#6D28D9` text and `#EDE9FE` background when selected.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** 44px height, `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, `0.5rem` border radius.
- **Focus State:** `1.5px solid #6D28D9` accompanied by a soft halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.15)`.
- **Currency Inputs:** Prefixed with static `KSh` label in `#6B7280`, input text rendered in bold tabular figures.

### Cards & Ledger Rows
- **Card Containers:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, `1rem` radius. Header areas feature `title-md` or `headline-sm` alongside contextual quick actions.
- **Data & Appointment Lists:** Alternating or bordered rows with subtle `#F9FAFB` hover transitions. Stylist assignment rows feature avatar thumbnails with `0.5rem` soft rounding.

### Point-of-Sale (POS) & Touch Controls
- **Touch NumPad & Quick Cash Tiles:** High-contrast tiles with 56px height, subtle `#F3F4F6` borders, and instant visual depression feedback.
- **Running Ticket Sidebar:** Grounded with a clean header, scrollable itemized bill with service staff attribution, and a high-emphasis checkout card calculating subtotal, discounts, and total in prominent `KSh` notation.
